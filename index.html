02 · The Offer Stack

Free up front.
$1,500 to get in.

The top of the funnel is generous. The paywall sits between the discovery call and the blood draw. That order shapes every conversion number that follows.

Free, top of funnel

Practice Guide + $100 voucher

Toward the first visit, captured by email. Plus a free 20 to 30 minute discovery call with a practice advisor, and a free Vitality lab test framed as an $842 value: 22 markers, 9 sex hormones, 8 thyroid, 5 vitamins and minerals. The test is free only with the paid onboarding, gated behind a see if you qualify quiz.

Core paid offer

$1,500 all-in onboarding

80 plus biomarker panel, InBody 770 body scan, provider prep, and a 60 minute provider consultation. The $100 voucher applies here. No insurance. HSA and FSA accepted, plus CareCredit and Cherry financing.

Ongoing revenue

Follow-ups and monthly care

Follow-up visits $275 each. Hormone therapy $70 to $200 a month. GLP-1 weight loss meds through compounding pharmacies, $250 to $500 a month. Supplements $20 to $100 a month.

03 · The Three Demographics

Three patients.
Three different buys.

Med Matrix publishes no demographics. These three are deduced from the offer stack, the ad themes, and the clinic's own patient stories. Shared thread: Maine, New Hampshire, and Massachusetts, cash-pay, middle to upper-middle class, and told their labs were normal.

1 · The core

Women 40 to 60

Perimenopause and menopause. The highest-value patient. She buys all three profit engines at once: HRT at $70 to $200 a month for years, GLP-1 weight loss, and Botox. A dedicated menopause quiz funnel is built for her.

Stories: Stacey in her 50s, Joy with six years of menopause and no sleep, Heidi at 55, Elizabeth at 60.

2 · The volume

Women 30 to 45

Weight loss, energy, early hormones. Weight loss is the biggest ad theme, and the weight-loss quiz funnel is built for her. She comes in for weight and fatigue, then converts into hormones and aesthetics over time.

Stories: Ashley, three years postpartum and still not herself. Brianna, crashing every afternoon at 40. The gained 43 pounds in six months cases.

3 · The stickiest

Men 35 to 55

Low testosterone, weight, performance. Smallest of the three, but the stickiest. TRT patients stay on therapy indefinitely once stabilized. The clinic's own LinkedIn content speaks directly to men in their 30s and 40s about flat energy, brain fog, and low drive.

Stories: 70 pounds down, 37 pounds down, Mathew down 25.

How to read it: Conversions are leads from clicks. L2C Rate is lead to customer. Customers are Conversions times L2C Rate. CPA is spend per lead. AOV is the 12-month value per patient, onboarding plus the ongoing mix. Revenue is the 12-month cohort. ROAS is Revenue divided by spend. CAC is spend per enrolled patient.

State clearly: these are benchmarks from public sources, not Med Matrix internal data. The funnel is a 4 to 12 week prospect-to-booking cycle, so monthly patients are starts, not same-month cash.
